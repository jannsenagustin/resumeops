# Sprint 4B — Enterprise Experience

## Objective

Present verified enterprise delivery as an engineering profile rather than a resume-style employment block.

## Changes

- Structured professional content by engineering domain, technical focus, client delivery, leadership, and technology group.
- Documented approximately seven years of Splunk administration and development within more than eight years at Accenture.
- Represented selected global client delivery while keeping Accenture as the employer of record.
- Added leadership, mentoring, internal training, and reusable-asset contributions.

## Technical Decisions

- Store experience in typed data rather than embedding it in JSX.
- Describe responsibilities with verifiable operational language.
- Keep nontechnical relocation history in Career Journey.
- Exclude confidential implementation details, unsupported metrics, and unverified architecture claims.

## Challenges

- Converting detailed professional history into concise, scannable evidence.
- Preserving client context without implying direct employment by client organizations.
- Showing technical breadth without overstating ownership or outcomes.

## Lessons Learned

- Clear data categories make professional experience easier to verify and maintain.
- Employer attribution and status language protect credibility.
- Technical detail is valuable only when it remains supportable in an interview.

## Review Outcome

The review retained the engineering-domain structure and required factual wording, semantic improvements, and clear employer attribution. Lint and production build validation passed.

Included in the Sprint 4 platform milestone; not recorded as a separately versioned release in the current changelog.
