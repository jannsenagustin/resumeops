# Sprint 4D — Brand & Content Refinement

## Objective

Position ResumeOps around Observability Engineering while preserving Splunk Enterprise as the verified specialization and keeping every professional statement supportable.

## Changes

- Updated the primary identity to Observability Engineer.
- Added the supporting line Specializing in Splunk Enterprise.
- Introduced the official tagline: Turning operational data into engineering insight.
- Replaced unsupported design-oriented Hero wording with administration, enhancement, automation, and support language.
- Updated browser metadata and current documentation.
- Reframed ResumeOps as an engineering platform for future observability projects.

## Technical Decisions

- Treat the tagline as a single brand statement rather than repeated body copy.
- Separate professional identity from specialization.
- Describe actual responsibilities rather than infer duties from certifications.
- Preserve existing Hero layout and responsive behavior during content refinement.

## Challenges

- Broadening the professional position without inflating the role.
- Removing contradictory legacy terminology across current documentation.
- Keeping branding concise while retaining verified enterprise detail.

## Lessons Learned

- Certification scope and professional responsibility are different forms of evidence.
- Precise verbs make experience more credible and easier to defend.
- Brand consistency requires auditing metadata and documentation as well as visible copy.

## Review Outcome

The final review confirmed one rendered tagline, consistent identity and specialization, no unsupported enterprise-architecture claims, and unchanged Hero layout. Lint, production build, and diff validation passed.

## Release

Recorded as the final content-refinement stage of the Sprint 4 platform milestone.
