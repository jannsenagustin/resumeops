# ADR 0002: Career Journey Separate from Professional Experience

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The user's background includes Accenture technical experience as well as Walmart and Trenton roles following relocation to Canada.

## Decision

Professional Experience will focus on Accenture and future technical roles. Walmart and Trenton may appear in Career Journey as part of the user's relocation, resilience, and return-to-tech narrative.

## Consequences

Technical credibility remains focused while the full personal journey can still be represented honestly.
