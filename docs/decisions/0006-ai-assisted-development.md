# ADR 0006: AI-Assisted Development with Human Review

## Status

Accepted

## Context

ChatGPT and Codex are being used to accelerate planning, implementation, and review.

## Decision

Jannsen retains product ownership and final approval. ChatGPT supports architecture, prompt design, and review. Codex performs targeted implementation and validation.

## Consequences

Development can move faster while preserving human judgment, testing, accountability, and scope control.
