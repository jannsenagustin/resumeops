# ADR-001: ResumeOps Repositioned as an Observability Engineering Platform

## Status

Accepted

## Context

ResumeOps began as a resume-style portfolio and evolved into a system for documenting professional experience, engineering decisions, case studies, and planned Splunk labs. Earlier role-focused language was too narrow for that direction and risked presenting the site as a conventional resume.

The verified professional foundation is enterprise Splunk administration, development, support, troubleshooting, automation, and operational improvement. The platform needs to communicate that experience without implying unsupported ownership of enterprise architecture.

## Decision

Position Jannsen as an **Observability Engineer** with the supporting specialization **Specializing in Splunk Enterprise**.

Adopt the ResumeOps tagline:

> Turning operational data into engineering insight.

Use responsibility-based language for professional claims. ResumeOps will operate as an engineering portfolio and documentation platform for enterprise experience, engineering case studies, decisions, and production-style projects.

## Reasoning

- Observability Engineering describes the broader problem domain while Splunk Enterprise communicates the verified specialization.
- Responsibility-based wording is more credible and interview-defensible than inflated job titles.
- A stable brand statement gives future case studies and documentation a consistent purpose.
- The platform framing supports growth beyond a single website or isolated case study.

## Consequences

- Homepage, metadata, and current documentation use the new identity consistently.
- Splunk remains central without limiting future observability work.
- Planned projects must remain clearly distinguished from completed professional work.
- Architectural credentials may be documented as certifications, but must not be presented as evidence of unverified architecture ownership.
